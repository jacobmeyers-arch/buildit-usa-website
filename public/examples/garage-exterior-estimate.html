Scope of Work

Full exterior restoration of the detached garage: the original non-functional barn doors come out in favor of standard overhead doors, deteriorated siding and trim are replaced face by face, and the entire building is scraped, primed, and repainted.

  • Two overhead garage doors (8' × 7' standard panel, non-insulated) replacing the original barn doors
    • Bay openings reframed to square and size for tracks — included
    • Door openers and their electrical circuits carried as a priced allowance
  • Siding replacement (~185 SF) where deteriorated: west bottom course (replacing the temporary white-board patch), west upper sections, south face areas, north bottom
    • New building paper and flashing behind all replaced areas, profile matched to existing
  • Trim replacement — corners, rakes, gable ends (~96 LF) plus full door-trim rebuild at the north bays
  • Heavy scrape, prime, and two finish coats on all four faces, trim, and doors (~920 SF)
    • Pre-1978 paint handled by site practice — outdoor wet-scrape methods, protective equipment, isolation, and bagged disposal — priced into this estimate
  • Building permit (Town of Ballston building department) — included in the total
  • Mobilization, site protection, all fasteners, adhesives, and trim materials
  • Final cleanup and debris disposal
Not in This Scope
  • Window repair or replacement — the boarded openings on the south and east faces remain as-is, with siding patched around them. Adding window replacement later would run roughly $2,000–$5,000.
  • Gutters — the building has none, and that is the likely root cause of the bottom-course rot. A gutter add-on (roughly $800–$1,500) is recommended to protect the new siding investment.
  • Roof work, interior work, and structural repairs beyond the door-bay reframe — none identified as needed, none included.
