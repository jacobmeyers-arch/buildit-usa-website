Scope of Work

Both downstairs bathrooms are gutted to the studs and subfloor and rebuilt as one full bath and one half bath, with all-new plumbing and electrical throughout.

  • Demolition of both rooms to studs and subfloor, including the partition wall
    • Dust containment and floor protection through the occupied home
    • Hold-point inspection of the subfloor before rebuild — repair budget already included
  • Reconfigured layout — new partition per plan, blocking for vanities and accessories
  • All-new plumbing — supply and drain/waste/vent lines, 30×60 alcove tub, two toilets, two vanity hookups, pressure tested
  • Heat kept in both rooms — baseboard removal and reinstatement budgeted; type confirmed when walls open
  • All-new electrical — dedicated circuits, GFCI protection, vanity and ceiling lights, new exhaust fan
  • Insulation and vapor barrier at exterior walls, moisture-resistant drywall, full finish and prime
  • Tile floor in both rooms (~62 SF) plus tub surround (~50 SF) with waterproofing membrane
  • Vanities (30″ + 42″), tub, two toilets, faucets, and shower trim — builder-grade stock allowances
  • Trim, doors, and full paint
  • Building permit through the Town of Ballston building department — included in the price
  • All fasteners, adhesives, leveling compound, trim materials
  • Final cleanup and debris disposal
Not in This Scope
  • Finish upgrades beyond builder-grade stock allowances — moving to true mid-range tile, fixtures, and vanities adds roughly $3,900–$5,850 if selected
  • Specialty features — heated floor, shower niche/bench, frameless glass — not included
  • Adjacent laundry room — untouched beyond coordinating connections at the shared wall
  • Concrete or slab work — none included; the floor system is framed (2×8 joists) and plumbing routes through the joist bays

Sequence of Operations

2.Demo
Strip both rooms to studs + subfloor. Remove partition wall. Lead-safe site practice — isolation + PPE on scraped surfaces. Load out debris. (line 1)
3.Subfloor Hold Point
Inspect, probe for moisture under tub and at threshold. Spot repair per allowance. (line 11)
4.Framing
New partition wall per plan. Blocking for vanities, accessories. (line 2)
5.Heat Reroute
Drain down if hydronic; remove baseboard runs for rebuild, reinstate at trim-out. Plumber critical path. (line 12)
6.Plumbing Rough
All new supply + DWV through framed 2×8 joist bays. Set tub. Stub all fixtures. Pressure test. (line 3)
7.Electrical Rough
New circuits from panel. GFCIs, switches, lights, fan. Rough inspection. (line 4)
8.Insulation
Exterior walls where exposed. Vapor barrier per code. (line 5)
9.Drywall
Moisture-resistant in wet areas. Hang, tape, finish, prime. (line 6)
10.Tile
Backer + waterproofing in surround. Floor tile both rooms. Surround tile. Grout + seal. (line 7)
11.Fixtures & Trim-Out
Set vanities (line 8); set toilets, faucets, shower trim, final connections (line 9).
12.Trim & Paint
Base, casing, doors. Paint walls/ceiling/trim. Caulk transitions. Lead-safe prep practice on any sanding. (line 10)
13.Final Punch
Test everything. Touch-up. Clean. Walkthrough. (line 13)
